What counts as a "session"?
A session starts when a user opens the MemorieAI panel and ends when they close it or after 30 minutes of inactivity. One visitor talking through multiple tasks = one session.
Can I use my own Vapi assistant?
Yes. Pro and Enterprise plans let you connect your own Vapi assistant ID via the dashboard. Pass it through the data-key attribute on the script tag.
Do you offer discounts for government or nonprofits?
Yes — municipalities, public universities, and registered nonprofits qualify for 40% off Pro plans. Reach out to sales with proof of status.
What happens when I exceed my session limit?
We'll notify you at 80% and 100%. On Starter, the bubble pauses until the next billing cycle. On Pro, you can enable auto-overage billing at $0.02/session.
